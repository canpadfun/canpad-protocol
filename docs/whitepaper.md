# Canpad Whitepaper

## Vision

A Solana market view that is quick to read and honest about what it knows. One screen, ten mints, no decoration standing between a person and the numbers.

## Mission

Turn public market data into a calm, legible board. Rank by turnover, show depth next to price, keep the last good reading when a source stumbles, and never present a fact the project has not stated.

## Principles

- Every external fact is a single record with a state: stated, absent or unconfirmed.
- Unstated records render inert and carry no value and no link.
- The market feed is independent of the contract address. The address being absent never dims the board.
- Motion serves reading, and stops entirely for visitors who ask for less of it.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: The board

Ship the live board: ranked mints, price, change, turnover and depth, copy per mint, ticker, resilient polling.

### Phase 2: The register

Publish the contract address and social links as they are stated, and wire each one into the page through its register record.

### Phase 3: Depth of view

Add richer filters and per-mint detail panels built on the same two public sources.

### Phase 4: Community surface

Open the code, document the register pattern, and invite contributions.
